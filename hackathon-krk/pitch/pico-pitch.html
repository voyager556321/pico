For companies

  • Quality is uncertain until after delivery

For workers

  • Payment can depend on the client’s subjective decision
  • Completed work does not always mean guaranteed payment

Every task is funded before anyone starts working.

PICO05 / Matching

No applications. No portfolios. No manual selection.

  • The platform sends the task to workers with the required verified skill.
  • Interested workers take a short task-specific qualification test generated by the platform.
  • The first qualified worker gets the task.
Skill gate + qualify race, not CV browsePico
PICO06 / Verification

The client is not the only person deciding payment.

After submission, an independent verified specialist checks the work against predefined Acceptance Criteria.

Pass

Worker gets paid from locked escrow.

Needs revision

Worker fixes and resubmits.

The verifier is paid for the review itself — not for choosing a specific verdict.

Independent QA, not client moodPico
PICO07 / Why Solana

Solana makes the payment logic programmable.

Smart contracts manage

  • Escrow lock before work
  • Payment release on PASS
  • Verifier reward
  • Transparent settlement

Why it fits

USDC funds are locked before work begins. Fast transactions and low fees make this suitable for task-based payments.

Programmable trust, not a custodial spreadsheetPico
